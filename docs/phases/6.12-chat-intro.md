# 6.12 — The chat intro scrolls away

## What you reported

> The chat header … is too large and should scroll away to leave more space for
> the chat messages.

## What it was

The hero — eyebrow, headline and the paragraph about answering only from the
documents — lived in `page.tsx` as a `shrink-0` block *above* `AskBox`. Outside
the scroller, it kept its full height forever: a ten-question conversation was
reading through a letterbox.

It now sits at the top of the scroll region, so it is the beginning of the
transcript. It is on screen when you arrive, scrolls out of the way as you ask,
and scrolls back if you go looking for it. The composer stays pinned, as of 6.1.

## The one risk, and why it is a test rather than a promise

6.2 and 6.11 both came down to the same rule: the scroller recognises a prepend
by the element that *used to be first* having moved down, so anything permanently
pinned at the top of `MessageScrollerContent` stops it recognising prepends at
all — and an infinite-scroll list then refetches until it is exhausted. Putting
the hero at the top of the content is precisely that shape.

It is safe here because the intro renders only when `hasOlder` is false: the home
page never pages backwards, and a conversation with older messages to fetch never
shows it. But "safe because of how it happens to be used today" is what caused
6.11, so `message-list.test.tsx` asserts it directly — with older messages
available, the intro is absent and a real message is the first child. Removing
the guard fails two of its three tests.

## Verify

- Ask several questions on `/`: the intro scrolls up out of the way, the messages
  get the space, the composer stays put.
- Scroll back to the top: the intro is there.
- Open a long conversation from History: no intro, and paging still works.
