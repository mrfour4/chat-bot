## A test the log found for me

Smoke-testing after 6.8 turned up a runtime error in the dev log:

```text
MISSING_MESSAGE: Could not resolve `documents.openDocument` in messages for locale `vi`
```

It was transient — I wrote the component before adding the key — but the suite
should have caught it and could not. `messages.test.ts` proves the two
catalogues *agree*, and both agreed the key did not exist.

So `keys.test.ts` scans the source for literal `t("…")` calls, resolves the
variable back to its `useTranslations("ns")` namespace, and requires each key to
exist in both files. Falsified by deleting `openDocument` from both catalogues:
the parity test passes and the new one names the file.

That is §9 again — a half-translated catalogue looks exactly like a working one
until someone loads that page in that language.

## Written down in CONVENTION.md

Five rules that would otherwise have to be rediscovered:

- Nothing permanent may sit at the top of `MessageScrollerContent` (6.2).
- `documents.updated_by` is stamped by a trigger, not by routes (6.4).
- Only upload, restore, retry and cron may call Gemini; `GEMINI_BASE_URL` makes
  it unreachable rather than merely unused (6.3).
- A tooltip on a disabled control goes on the wrapper (6.5).
- `Select.Value` reads the Root's `items`; `truncate` needs `table-fixed` and
  `max-w-0` (6.8, 6.9).
